# LexisNexis Risk Solutions

> LexisNexis Risk Solutions is the risk-data and analytics arm of RELX, headquartered in Alpharetta, Georgia. In United States insurance it does not underwrite risk; it sells the contributory databases and scores that carriers rate against — C.L.U.E. Auto claims history, Current Carrier prior-coverage verification, Motor Vehicle Records across all fifty states, Driver Discovery undisclosed-driver detection, InsurView public-records attributes and VIN Services vehicle data — alongside telematics exchange, data prefill, fraud detection, claims and life-insurance underwriting products. Its API surface is contract-first and partner-gated: a real first-party developer portal exists at developer.lexisnexisrisk.com (a SwaggerHub Portal instance) and publicly enumerates six insurance API products, but every product page redirects an anonymous visitor to a login. No OpenAPI, base URL, scope list, webhook catalog or Postman collection is published to the open web.

This file was generated by API Evangelist from the public LexisNexis Risk Solutions surface and the artifacts in this repository. It is not published by LexisNexis Risk Solutions.

## Access posture

- Gated: the six insurance API products are documented only behind a portal login; credentials are issued under commercial contract.
- The only anonymously reachable machine-readable LexisNexis Risk Solutions surface is the Atlassian Statuspage v2 API at https://status.lexisnexisrisk.com/api/v2/summary.json
- The only first-party client libraries published to public registries are for LexisNexis Emailage, the email/IP fraud-risk product.
- Distribution into carrier systems runs through policy-administration marketplaces (Guidewire PolicyCenter) rather than through an open API.

## APIs (documented, login-gated)

- [C.L.U.E. Auto](https://developer.lexisnexisrisk.com/clue_auto): Up to seven years of personal automobile claim history matching the search criteria. Portal slug `clue_auto`.
- [C.L.U.E. Auto with Current Carrier](https://developer.lexisnexisrisk.com/clue_auto_cc): Claim history plus identification of existing or prior policy carriers. Portal slug `clue_auto_cc`.
- [Driver Discovery](https://developer.lexisnexisrisk.com/driver_discovery): Identifies potentially undisclosed drivers residing at an applicant's address. Portal slug `driver_discovery`.
- [InsurView](https://developer.lexisnexisrisk.com/insurview): Public-records and alternative-data attributes complementing traditional insurance scoring. Portal slug `insurview`.
- [Motor Vehicle Record (MVR)](https://developer.lexisnexisrisk.com/mvr): Driver history evaluated consistently across all fifty states. Portal slug `mvr`.
- [VIN Services](https://developer.lexisnexisrisk.com/vin_services): Vehicle registration and title data keyed on the VIN. Portal slug `vin_services`.
